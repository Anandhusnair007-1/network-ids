## 2. Withheld-Category Experiment ($N = 2,179$ Unseen Attack Flows)

From `reports/eval_results.json` (`withheld_experiment` and `policy_comparison.*.withheld`):

| Withheld Attack Class | Total Flows | RF Detected (Misclassified) | IF Detected ($<0.061102$) | Hybrid Baseline (`all_alerts`) | Hybrid Tiered Actionable (`HIGH+MEDIUM`) | Hybrid Balanced (`t=0.0`) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Infiltration** | 36 | **0** (0.0%) | **31** (86.11%) | **31** (86.11%) | **24** (66.67%) | **28** (77.78%) |
| **Web Attack – Brute Force** | 1,470 | 69 (4.69%) | 74 (5.03%) | 143 (9.73%) | 140 (9.52%) | 141 (9.59%) |
| **Web Attack – XSS** | 652 | 21 (3.22%) | 18 (2.76%) | 39 (5.98%) | 37 (5.67%) | 37 (5.67%) |
| **Web Attack – Sql Injection** | 21 | 4 (19.05%) | 0 (0.0%) | 4 (19.05%) | 4 (19.05%) | 4 (19.05%) |
| **Total Withheld** | **2,179** | **94** (4.31%) | **123** (5.64%) | **217** (9.96%) | **205** (9.41%) | **210** (9.64%) |

**Key Empirical Findings:**
1. Random Forest was 100% blind to `Infiltration` (0/36 detected; predicted all 36 as `BENIGN`), whereas Isolation Forest flagged 31/36 (`86.11%`) under `baseline` (24/36 `MEDIUM` + 7/36 `LOW` under `tiered`).
2. Flow-level statistical features failed to capture HTTP-layer web attacks (`Web Attack` detection stayed at 6%–19%), proving payload/WAF inspection is required for Layer-7 attacks.
3. Unsupervised detection introduces a clear precision-recall trade-off: `Baseline` raises FPR from `0.26%` to `9.42%` (+38,405 false alarms), which `Tiered` cuts to `3.21%` actionable FPR while keeping `66.7%` of `Infiltration` in `MEDIUM` and the rest in `LOW`.

## 3. Inference Time, Alert Compression & Test Suite Status

- **Inference Latency & Throughput (Discrepancy Across Docs — No Standalone Benchmark Script in `src/`):**
  - `README.md` (L151–153): reports **12.16 microseconds/flow** (~82,204 flows/sec) and ~280 MB peak memory on Intel Xeon @ 2.20 GHz.
  - `TECHNICAL_PROJECT_REPORT.md` (§11.4): reports **15.79 microseconds/flow** (~63,330 flows/sec) on a 10,000-flow batch (`5.74 ms` scaler, `55.88 ms` RF, `57.32 ms` IF, `157.89 ms` total).
  - `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` (L41): notes ~47,000 flows/s on 2 CPU cores.
  - *Audit Finding:* No dedicated reproducible latency-benchmark script was found in `src/` or `tests/`.
- **Alert Grouping Compression:**
  - `TECHNICAL_PROJECT_REPORT.md` (§11.5) reported `99.56%` reduction (2,483 alerts -> 11 groups on a 10k sample) *before* `summarize_groups()` was hardened in `src/alert_grouper.py` (`L285–305`).
  - `README.md` (L81) reports measured compression with the updated `summarize_groups()` formula: **8%** on a synthetic 2,000-flow upload with random IPs/2s spacing, and **95%** on a shuffled sample with batch-wide grouping.
- **Confusion Matrices & Pending Experiments:**
  - `evaluate_hybrid.py` computes binary confusion matrices internally (`eval_binary`), and `classification_report` is logged to stdout, but neither the confusion matrix arrays nor per-class test tables are persisted to `reports/eval_results.json`.
  - Live network traffic scoring, Docker Compose load tests, and live `pytest` execution under the broken `.venv` remain **pending** (see `07_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` and `09_GAPS_AND_NEXT_STEPS.md`).
